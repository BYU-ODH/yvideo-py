Y Video lets BYU faculty and their teaching assistants customize videos for their courses. You can skip, mute, or blur scenes, add comments, and edit subtitles to cater videos to your students. And you don’t need any video editing experience to get started.

If you’re new to this version of Y Video, start with Getting started for a quick walkthrough of the basics.
