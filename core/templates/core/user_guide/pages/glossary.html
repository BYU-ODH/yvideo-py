Terms

Anchor Point
A moment on the timeline where the position and size of a Blur Area is saved. Often called a “keyframe” in other editors.
Archive
Hides a playlist from your active list without deleting it. Archived items appear under Archive on Home.
Collaborator
A person who can edit a playlist.
Original resource
The unedited source video. Overlays never change it.
Overlay
A named set of edits that changes how the media player shows an original resource, without modifying the video file.
Playlist
A collection of videos that you can publish to course sections or share with individuals.
Properties Panel
The right-hand panel of the editor that shows the adjustable settings of the selected tool item.
Publish
Makes a playlist or video visible to the students and individuals listed in Publishing Details.
Scrub Head
The marker showing the current location in the video, synced between the timeline and the Overlay Preview.
Subtitle track
A set of subtitles, extracted from the original resource or created in the Subtitle Editor.
Timecode
How far into the video the Scrub Head is.
Tool
One of the seven ways to modify media: Skip, Mute, Blank, Pause, Blur, Comment, and Clip.
Tool item
An individual use of a tool, shown as a colored block on the timeline.
Track
A layer on the timeline used to organize tool items.
