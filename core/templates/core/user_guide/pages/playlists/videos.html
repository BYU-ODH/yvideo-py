Add a new video to your library

  1. Go to Your Videos

    Your Videos is a collection of all the videos you have ever added or edited.

    {% include "core/user_guide/partials/screenshot.html" with caption="Your Videos page" wide=True %}

  2. Select “+ Add New Video”

    Follow the prompts to choose the original resource.

    {% include "core/user_guide/partials/screenshot.html" with caption="“+ Add New Video” button" %}

Add or remove videos in a playlist

  1. Open the playlist and select “Add/Remove Videos”

    The button is in the row of actions below the playlist description.

    {% include "core/user_guide/partials/screenshot.html" with caption="Playlist action buttons" wide=True %}

  2. Choose the videos to include

    Select videos to add them, or deselect them to remove them from the playlist. Removing a video from a playlist does not delete it from Your Videos.

    {% include "core/user_guide/partials/screenshot.html" with caption="Add/Remove Videos selector" wide=True %}

  3. Select “Save Changes”

    Each video now appears in the Videos list with its own Published toggle.

    {% include "core/user_guide/partials/screenshot.html" with caption="Videos list on the playlist page" wide=True %}
