Follow these steps to set up your first playlist and share edited videos with your students. For details on any step, see the rest of this guide.

Publish your first playlist

  1. Create a playlist

    Everything in Y Video starts with a playlist, which is a collection of one or more videos. You need a playlist before you can add or edit videos, and your playlist settings also control who can see your videos and who can collaborate with you.

  2. Add videos

    Click Add New Video, then select Create from existing resource. If the video isn’t in Y Video yet, select Request a new resource and fill out the form. An administrator will ask you to verify that you or the Library owns the video. You will then be notified when it’s ready for use. You can also add a video from YouTube by pasting the YouTube URL into the YouTube URL text field.

  3. Edit your video

    From your playlist, open the video’s details page and click Edit your video to open the Overlay Editor. Your edits are overlays: they change how a student sees the video, but the original file is never altered.

    • Overlay tools: Add any of the seven tools (Skip, Mute, Blank, Pause, Blur, Comment, and Clip) to the timeline, then adjust each one using its properties.
    • Subtitles: Edit the video’s original subtitle track or create a new one from scratch.

  4. Publish your playlist

    When your videos are ready, go to the Playlist Details page and assign the playlist to specific courses or individuals, such as students auditing the class.

  5. Collaborate (optional)

    To work with TAs or other faculty, you can assign individuals to be collaborators on a playlist, or export and import specific overlays.
