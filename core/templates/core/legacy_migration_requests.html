Before you submit

For an entire collection, go to the old Y-Video's Collection Manager and select the desired collection on the left side of the page. Click "Edit User Access" and remove any courses or individuals that are no longer relevant. Then copy the URL from your browser's address bar and paste it below.

For an individual video resource, go to the video's player or editor page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.
