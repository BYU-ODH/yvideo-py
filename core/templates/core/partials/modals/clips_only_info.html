When this setting is turned on, viewers can only watch the parts of this video that are defined as clips in the video editor. Playback pauses at the end of each clip, and seeking outside a clip moves to the nearest clip boundary.

Showing only the portions of a video needed for instruction gives better Fair Use protection when using copyrighted material.

If no clips are defined, turning this setting on will prevent viewers from being able to watch any part of the video.
