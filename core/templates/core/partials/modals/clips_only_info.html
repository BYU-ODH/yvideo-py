When this setting is on, viewers can only watch the parts of this video that are defined as clips in the video editor. Playback skips everything outside of those clips, and seeking outside a clip jumps into the nearest clip.

Showing only the portions of a video needed for instruction gives better Fair Use protection when using copyrighted material.

If no clips are defined, selecting this box will prevent viewers from being able to watch any part of the video.
